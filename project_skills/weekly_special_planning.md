# Weekly Special Planning Skill

Purpose: turn one weekly theme into a coherent weekend special instead of a one-off topic dump.

## When To Use

Use this skill for weekly or weekend projects such as:

- Copper vs market heat.
- AI cycle continuation.
- Commodity / macro linkage to Taiwan stocks.
- A week-in-review theme that should not be forced into the daily report.

## Core Rule

The topic must connect to Taiwan stocks.

Do not leave the episode at the commodity or macro layer.

## Planning Requirements

Each weekly special should define:

- The main thesis.
- The data series to inspect.
- The Taiwan stock or sector mapping.
- The failure condition.
- The follow-up question for next week.

## Good Weekly Frames

- "AI 熱潮還在嗎？"
- "銅價在說什麼？"
- "景氣熱度有沒有真的回來？"
- "哪一類台股最先反應？"

## Bad Weekly Frames

- Pure market commentary without a Taiwan stock mapping.
- A fear headline without data support.
- A one-day news recap that does not need a weekly format.

## Output Contract

The skill must produce:

- A weekly thesis.
- One supporting data story.
- One Taiwan stock / sector bridge.
- One clear verification point for the following week.
- A title direction that can be adapted into YouTube packaging.
